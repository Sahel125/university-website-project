About Vantaspace

Pioneering the future of space exploration

What is Vantaspace

Vantaspace is a publicly funded organisation that has worked with numerous different space branches such as NASA, ESA, and other international space agencies to help provide the guidance, research and technology needed for visting celestial bodies and beyond!

We have been at the forefront of space exploration for over two decades, pushing the boundaries of what's possible in deep space research, exploration and enhancements within technology.

Why we matter

Vantaspace is the bridge between those who are interested in deep space exploration and the high-precision space agencies around the globe. We help many people share knowledge in the field of helping Earth's ability to discover the endless scope of the universe.

Our collaborative approach ensures that knowledge and resources are shared across borders, accelerating humanities progress in space exploration and making the cosmos more accessible to everyone regardless of age, gender or identity.

Our duty

To advance mankinds presence in space through innovation, collaboration and sustainable exploration.

Our vision

A future where deep space exploration is accessible, sustainable and beneficial for all of humanity.
